T-Dongle firmware

Flash your T-Dongle

Turns a LILYGO T-Dongle-S3 (the one with the screen) into a USB Ethernet adapter that joins Wi-Fi by itself. One firmware, two modes: a transparent Wi-Fi bridge (the default) and a tailnet gateway. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Project page

This browser cannot talk to USB devices. Use desktop Chrome or Edge.

First install

  1. Hold the dongle's button while you plug it into USB, then let go.
  2. Click Install, pick the port (it may be called "USB JTAG/serial debug unit"), and allow erase.
  3. When it finishes, unplug the dongle and plug it back in without the button.

Update

Already running T-Dongle firmware? No button needed.

  1. With the dongle plugged in, click Prepare for update and pick the "T-Dongle-S3" port. The dongle restarts into its loader.
  2. Click Install above, pick the new port, and decline erase so your saved Wi-Fi networks are kept.

Add a Wi-Fi network

After flashing, the dongle needs a Wi-Fi network to join (2.4 GHz). Plug it in without the button, then enter the network here. Nothing leaves your computer: it goes straight to the dongle over USB. You can save up to 8.

Networks can also be added or removed later from the Android companion app or the serial console. Details are in the README.
