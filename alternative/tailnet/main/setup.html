About this preview

Memberships and identities are saved on the dongle. Active memberships depend on available memory. This preview routes IPv4 TCP and UDP peer traffic; registration alone does not verify that a peer is reachable.

Disconnect keeps the identity for later. Remove deletes the saved membership and identity. Original bridge settings remain saved when installing this alternative firmware.
